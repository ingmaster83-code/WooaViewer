Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between .eml and .msg?

.eml is the standard email format (MIME) and .msg is Microsoft Outlook's proprietary format. This viewer handles .msg only. Use the EML Viewer for .eml files.

Can I download attachments?

The current version displays the attachment list only. Downloading attachments is not supported.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.

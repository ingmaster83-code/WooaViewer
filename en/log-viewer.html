Frequently Asked Questions

What log formats are supported?

Any text-based log file is supported. ERROR, WARN, INFO, and DEBUG keywords are auto-detected regardless of case.

What if my log levels aren't detected?

Keywords are detected case-insensitively. For custom formats, use the keyword search to filter.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.

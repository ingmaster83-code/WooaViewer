Frequently Asked Questions

Can I open password-protected HWP files?

Password-protected files cannot be opened. Please remove the password in Hancom Office (Hangul) first.

Is all formatting perfectly displayed?

Most text, tables, images, and formatting render accurately, but very complex objects or special features may look slightly different. Use this for content preview purposes.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely within your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server. This zero-server architecture keeps your data 100% private, safe for sensitive documents and enterprise security environments.

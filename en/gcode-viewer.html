Frequently Asked Questions

Which slicers are supported?

All slicers that produce standard GCode are supported, including Cura, PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, and Simplify3D.

What if the file is very large?

Very large files may load slowly depending on your browser's performance. Consider previewing only a few layers at a time.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.

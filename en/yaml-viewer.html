Frequently Asked Questions

What if the YAML is invalid?

A parsing error with the location is shown. Check for correct indentation and colon usage.

Are both tab and space indentation supported?

Per the YAML standard, only space indentation is supported. Tab indentation will cause a parsing error.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely within your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server. This zero-server architecture keeps your data 100% private, safe for sensitive documents and enterprise security environments.

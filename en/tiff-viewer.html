💡 Tips

  • Multi-page TIFF files can be navigated with Previous/Next buttons.
  • Use Save as PNG to download the current page.
  • Commonly used for medical images, scanned documents, and fax files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I save all pages of a multi-page TIFF at once?

Currently, pages can be saved as PNG one at a time. Batch saving is not supported.

Can I open very high-resolution TIFFs?

High-resolution images may take longer to load depending on your browser's memory.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.

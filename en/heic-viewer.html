Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert HEIC to JPG?

Yes, after viewing the image, click 'Save as JPG/PNG' to download the converted file.

Can I open photos from Android devices?

HEIC/HEIF is mainly used by Apple devices. Android photos are usually JPG/PNG and don't need conversion.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.

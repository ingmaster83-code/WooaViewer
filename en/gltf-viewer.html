Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between GLTF and GLB?

GLTF is a JSON-based format with separate texture and binary files, while GLB packs everything into a single binary file. If your model has textures, GLB is recommended.

Can I open a GLTF that references external textures?

A .gltf that references separate texture or .bin files can't have those companion files uploaded alongside it, so it may load without textures or fail entirely. We recommend exporting as .glb, which bundles everything into one file.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why aren't images displaying?

Embedded images have partial support in the current version. If images don't appear, open the file directly in LibreOffice.

Can I open ODP (presentation) files?

Currently ODT (documents) and ODS (spreadsheets) are supported. ODP support is not yet available.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely within your browser — your files are never uploaded to any server. This zero-server architecture keeps your data 100% private, safe for sensitive documents and enterprise security environments.

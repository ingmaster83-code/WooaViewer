Frequently Asked Questions

Can I open password-protected DOCX files?

Password-protected files cannot be opened. Please remove the password in Word first.

Is all formatting perfectly displayed?

Complex layouts, custom fonts, and charts may not render perfectly. Use this for content preview purposes.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I open a file with multiple contacts?

Yes, multi-contact vCard files are supported and all contacts are displayed as individual cards.

Can I edit or save contacts?

The current viewer is view-only. For editing, use your phone's contacts app or Outlook.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.

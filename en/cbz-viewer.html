💡 Tips

  • CBZ (Comic Book ZIP) is simply a ZIP archive containing image files. You can rename it to .zip and open it with any archive tool.
  • Click any page image to view it enlarged. Click the dark background to close the zoom view.
  • Up to 50 pages are displayed. For longer comics, only the first 50 pages are loaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I open CBR files too?

CBR uses RAR compression, which is not currently supported. Only CBZ (ZIP-based) files are supported. To open a CBR, convert it to CBZ using a tool like Calibre or CDisplay Ex first.

The page order looks wrong.

Pages are sorted by the filename inside the CBZ archive. Filenames with numbers should sort correctly automatically. If the order is wrong, check the internal filenames of the CBZ.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your file is never sent to any server — it is completely private and safe.

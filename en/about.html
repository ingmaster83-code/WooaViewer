About WooaViewer

Free online viewer for 36 file formats — open files directly in your browser

👁️ What is WooaViewer?

WooaViewer is a free online file viewer service that lets you open a wide variety of file formats — Word, Excel, AutoCAD, Photoshop, and more — directly in your web browser without any software installation.

Simply drag and drop your file or click to select it, and instantly view its contents. All processing happens entirely within your browser, so your files are never sent to any server — completely safe and private.

⚡ Key Features

  • 🔒 Complete Privacy — Files are never sent to or stored on any server. All processing stays in your browser.
  • 🚀 Fast Processing — No waiting for server uploads. Files are processed locally at full speed.
  • 💸 Completely Free — All features are available free of charge. No account, no login, no payment required.
  • 📱 Works on Any Device — Fully responsive design for PC, laptop, tablet, and smartphone.
  • 🌐 No Installation — Just open your browser and go. Nothing to download or install.

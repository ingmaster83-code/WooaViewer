💡 Tips

  • Files are processed entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server
  • Type a page number and press Enter to jump to that page
  • Use the − / + buttons or the dropdown to adjust zoom level
  • Password-protected PDFs cannot be opened
  • Large PDFs with many pages may take a moment to render

FAQ

Why won't my PDF open?

Password-protected or corrupted PDFs cannot be opened. Try removing the password first with WooaPDF, or use a different file.

Is my file uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your file is never sent to any server. It's completely private.

Can I copy text or print?

This viewer renders pages as canvas images, so text selection is not supported. To print, use your browser's built-in print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P).

Need to edit or convert the PDF?

WooaPDF offers free tools to merge, split, compress, and convert PDFs — no sign-up needed.

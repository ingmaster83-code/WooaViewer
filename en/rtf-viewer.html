Frequently Asked Questions

Can I view images embedded in an RTF file?

Text content is displayed, but images embedded in RTF are not currently shown.

Is formatting (bold, color, etc.) preserved?

The current version extracts plain text without formatting. Use Word or LibreOffice to view with formatting.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.

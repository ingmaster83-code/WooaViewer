Frequently Asked Questions

Are recurring events displayed?

Events with a recurrence rule (RRULE) are shown once as the base event. Use a calendar app to see all occurrences.

Can I view files with many events?

Yes, all events in a .ics file are displayed, sorted by start date.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Files are never sent to any server.
